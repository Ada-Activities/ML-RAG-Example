Fountain Pen Ink Compatibility Guide

Fountain Pen Ink and Refill Compatibility

Choosing the right ink is crucial for the longevity and performance of your fountain pen. Using the wrong type of ink can cause irreversible clogging and damage to the pen's feed mechanism.

Cartridges vs. Converters:
- Proprietary Cartridges: Brands like Lamy, Pilot, and Platinum use proprietary ink cartridges. You must buy cartridges specific to that brand.
- Standard International Cartridges: Brands like Kaweco and Faber-Castell generally accept "Standard International" size cartridges.
- Converters: If your pen includes a converter, you can draw bottled ink directly into the pen, opening up your options to any fountain-pen-safe bottled ink.

Ink Safety Warning: You must only use inks specifically labeled as "Fountain Pen Ink." These are dye-based inks that dissolve completely in water.
- NEVER use Calligraphy Ink, India Ink, or Drawing Ink in a fountain pen. These contain shellac, acrylic, or heavy pigments that will permanently dry inside the capillary mechanisms of your fountain pen and ruin it.
- Shimmer Inks: Inks containing glitter or shimmer particles are safe for fountain pens but require regular cleaning (every 2 to 3 weeks) to prevent particulate buildup.
