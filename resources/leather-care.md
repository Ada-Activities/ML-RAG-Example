Leather Care and Maintenance Manual

Caring for Your Leather Journals and Folios

Our full-grain leather journals are designed to develop a beautiful patina over time. To ensure your leather goods last a lifetime, follow these maintenance guidelines:

Routine Conditioning: Leather can dry out and crack if left untreated. We recommend applying a high-quality leather conditioner or balm every 6 months using a soft, lint-free cloth. Work the conditioner in using circular motions and let it dry overnight.

Handling Scuffs and Scratches: Because we use untreated full-grain leather, superficial scuffs are normal. For light scratches, vigorously rub the affected area with the pad of your thumb; the natural oils from your skin and the friction heat will often buff out the scratch. For deeper scuffs, apply a small amount of leather balm.

Water Damage: If your leather folio gets wet, do not use a hairdryer or place it near a radiator, as forced heat will warp and crack the leather. Instead, gently blot the water with a dry towel and let the item air-dry naturally in a well-ventilated room.
