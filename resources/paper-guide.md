Understanding Paper Weight (GSM) and Texture

When selecting a notebook or notepad from our fine selection, understanding the GSM (Grams per Square Meter) will help you pair the right paper with your preferred writing instrument.

Standard Paper (70 - 80 GSM): Ideal for ballpoint pens, rollerballs, and standard graphite pencils. This weight is common in everyday notebooks. However, fountain pens and liquid-ink markers will likely experience "ghosting" (seeing the ink on the reverse side) or "bleed-through" (ink soaking completely through the page).

Premium Heavyweight Paper (100 - 120 GSM): This is the optimal weight for fountain pen users. Our 120 GSM paper is specially coated to resist feathering and bleed-through, ensuring crisp, clean lines for liquid inks.

Art and Watercolor Paper (200 - 300+ GSM): Designed for mixed media, heavy washes, and watercolor palettes. 300 GSM paper is thick, rigid, and highly absorbent.
- Hot Press: Smooth texture, ideal for detailed ink drawings and brush pens.
- Cold Press: Slightly textured/bumpy surface, best for traditional watercolors as it holds pigment well.
