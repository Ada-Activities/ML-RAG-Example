Fragile Shipping and Breakage Policy

We take great pride in safely delivering your delicate stationery items, particularly glass bottles of calligraphy ink, porcelain inkwells, and sealing wax melters.

Our Packaging Process: All glass and fragile items are double-wrapped in corrugated honeycomb paper and suspended in biodegradable packing peanuts to prevent shifting during transit. We do not use plastic bubble wrap in our facilities.

Reporting Broken Items: Despite our best efforts, glass items occasionally break during carrier transit. If you receive a shattered ink bottle or broken ceramic item:
1. Do not attempt to wash or salvage the broken glass, as this is a safety hazard.
2. Take a clear photograph of the damaged item inside its original shipping box.
3. Email the photograph to support within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp.

Once we receive your photograph, we will immediately dispatch a complimentary replacement via expedited 2-day shipping. You do not need to return the broken item to us; please dispose of it safely in your local glass recycling bin.
