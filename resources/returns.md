Custom Embossing Returns Policy

Return and Exchange Policy for Custom and Standard Items

We want you to love your stationery. For standard, non-customized items, we accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be in original, unused condition with all factory packaging intact.

Custom Embossed and Monogrammed Items: Because customized items are permanently altered specifically for you, we cannot accept returns, exchanges, or cancellations on any items that have been embossed, engraved, or monogrammed. This includes leather journals, custom-stamped wax seals, and personalized fountain pens.

Exceptions for Defective Custom Items: If your custom-embossed item arrives with a manufacturing defect, or if the monogramming does not match the exact text you provided in your order, you must contact our support team within 14 days of delivery. We will require photographic evidence of the defect. Upon verification, we will issue a full replacement of the item at no additional cost. We do not issue refunds for defective custom items; we only provide replacements.
